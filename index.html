How it works

This is a test of a betting model, run for fun. Every night the model compares FanDuel's player-prop prices with the rest of the market to find prices that look better than fair. A play makes this list only when the price looks good enough and the Sam score (0-100, built from the market edge, matchup, role, recent form and more) is 50 or higher. Model stakes are a quarter of the Kelly amount with caps per player, game and night. No money is placed by this site: every pick is graded on paper from the box score the next morning, and every result stays public, losses included.

Glossary

EV
Expected value: how much a bet should return on average at this price compared with its fair odds. +4% means about 4 cents per dollar over many bets.
CLV
Closing line value: how much better our price was than the final market price before tip-off. Beating the close is the clearest sign of real edge, more reliable than a short winning streak.
Units
How the model sizes its simulated plays: 1u = 1% of a bankroll. The model's records use flat 1u and its quarter-Kelly model stakes.
Sam score
ScuseBET's 0-100 rating. A = 80+, B = 65-79, C = 50-64. Below 50 is tracked but never bet.
Playable to
The worst FanDuel price where the bet is still about 2% better than fair. If FanDuel's price is worse than that, we show "line moved, skip".
